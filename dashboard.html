Privacy Policy

Data ownership. XBalance stores your profile and finance data in Supabase under your authenticated account.

Access controls. Row Level Security policies limit transactions, categories, and payment methods to their owner.

No selling of data. XBalance is a personal finance tracker and does not sell your financial data.

Account deactivation. When you deactivate your account, the profile is marked as deleted and associated finance records are permanently removed.

Your responsibility. Do not share your login credentials, and use a strong unique password.
